The persistent weakness in EcoPro reflects structural and operational pressures coursing through the Korean cathode and precursor ecosystem:

This inventory rationalization transmits directly upstream to EcoPro’s consolidated top line.
2. **Margin Compression from Metal Lag Effects:** The group's profitability remains sensitive to the pricing delta between feedstocks (lithium hydroxide, nickel sulfate) and contracted selling prices. 3. **Institutional De-risking:** The heavy foreign net outflow (-213,532 shares) reflects continued rotation away from capital-intensive, high-beta Korean EV components. With global battery demand displaying regional divergence—slower adoption in Europe and the United States contrasted with elevated domestic competition inside China—institutional investors have reduced portfolio weightings in upstream material suppliers that trade at premium historical multiples.
