Hanmi’s price behavior remains tightly tethered to the High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) capital expenditure cycle. As the market-dominant vendor of "DUAL TC Bonder" equipment—a specialized thermal compression bonder essential for vertically stacking DRAM dies in advanced packaging—Hanmi has historically captured a dominant share of tool shipments to key memory producers, most notably SK Hynix and Micron Technology.

The stock’s 14.71% 20-day recovery reflects stabilizing sentiment around 2026–2027 advanced packaging capacity additions. Additionally, market positioning is being reshaped by customer diversification efforts initiated over the 2024–2025 cycle, where Hanmi secured multi-hundred-billion won purchase orders from Micron in addition to its baseline demand from SK Hynix.
