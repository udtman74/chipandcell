## Fundamental Drivers: CNT Slurry Leverage and Dispersion Dominance

Nano Materials' sharp outperformance relative to its peer group reflects its differentiated exposure within the battery value chain, specifically in carbon nanotube (CNT) conductive additives:

1. **Insulation from Cathode Margin Squeezes:** While mainstream Korean cell and cathode manufacturers have struggled with inventory write-downs and raw-material pass-through lag—explaining the -8.11% median peer drop over 20 days—Nano Materials' exposure is centered on advanced functional additives. CNT slurries represent a small fraction of cell bill-of-materials cost but carry high technological entry barriers and stickier pricing.
2. **Single-Walled CNT (SWCNT) Market Position:** The company remains the preeminent commercial supplier of SWCNT dispersion slurries applied to silicon-dominant anodes. Because silicon anodes experience volumetric expansion exceeding 300% during lithiation, SWCNT dispersion is critical to maintaining electrical connectivity and cycle life. As next-generation EV platforms integrate higher silicon loadings (5–10%+) to improve charging speeds, demand for Nano Materials’ anode-grade slurries scales independently of pure cathode throughput.
3. **Capacity Additions Tracking Global Cell Footprints:** Nano Materials has strategically localized slurry manufacturing near major customer clusters, including dedicated capacity expansions in the United States and Europe to supply tier-1 Korean cell makers (such as LG Energy Solution and SK On). Institutional buyers have increasingly treated the company as an idiosyncratic play on next-generation battery chemistry rather than a commoditized volume play.
