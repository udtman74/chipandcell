## Risks and Caveats

While near-term momentum and institutional sponsorship are evident, several operational and cyclical risks warrant caution:

* **End-Market EV Demand Elasticity:** High-nickel cathode utilization remains tightly linked to North American and European EV adoption rates. Any further production pushbacks or vehicle trim cancellations by core OEM partners directly delay cathode off-take volumes.
* **Pricing Lag Effects:** Cathode pricing structures pass through underlying lithium and nickel benchmark prices with a multi-month lag. If upstream metal prices resume a downward trend, inventory valuation losses and ASP (average selling price) erosion could weigh on operating margins.
* **Capital Intensity and Free Cash Flow:** Large-scale capacity expansions across both cathode plants and synthetic graphite facilities require sustained capital expenditures. POSCO Future M must balance aggressive capacity deployment against leverage metrics in an elevated interest-rate environment.
