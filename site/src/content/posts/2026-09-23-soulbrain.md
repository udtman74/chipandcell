## Fundamental Drivers: Memory Utilization and Layer Scaling

Soulbrain is a core supplier of mission-critical wet chemicals—most notably high-purity hydrofluoric acid (HF), phosphoric acid ($H_3PO_4$) formulations, and chemical mechanical planarization (CMP) slurries—to Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix. Company-specific drivers supporting the re-rating include:

1. **3D NAND Layer-Count Scaling:** Soulbrain holds a dominant market share in high-selectivity phosphoric acid used for selective nitride stripping in 3D NAND fabrication. As memory manufacturers advance architectures beyond 200 and 300 vertical layers, etch times and chemical consumption per wafer rise disproportionately. Market expectations of higher bit-growth targets and fab utilization rates directly translate to consumable chemical volume expansions.
2. **Advanced DRAM and Packaging Consumables:** Demand for the company's CMP slurries and advanced cleaning solutions tracks leading-edge DRAM transitions (such as 1b-nm and 1c-nm nodes) and High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) stacking processes, where surface planarity and particle defect control require higher unit volumes of chemical consumables per processed wafer. 
3. **Operational Leverage over Capex Cycles:** Unlike front-end semiconductor equipment makers whose order books fluctuate heavily with capital expenditure cycles, Soulbrain’s revenue is tied primarily to wafer starts and fab operating rates. With utilization normalizing across Tier-1 domestic memory fabs, institutional flows appear to be targeting consumable material suppliers that offer earnings defensiveness combined with operating leverage.

## Risks and Caveats

- **Customer Concentration:** Soulbrain’s revenue remains heavily concentrated among domestic memory majors Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix. Any unilateral adjustment to fab production schedules or aggressive price renegotiations would compress operating margins.
- **Foreign Flow Divergence:** While institutional investors accumulated 23,975 shares today, foreign investors were net sellers of 6,877 shares, reflecting lingering caution among global accounts regarding non-hardware-direct AI exposure.
- **Feedstock Price Volatility:** Operating profitability in the chemical division remains exposed to input cost shifts, including market pricing for anhydrous hydrofluoric acid and raw phosphate rock, which cannot always be passed immediately into contract selling prices.
