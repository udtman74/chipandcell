Korea exports two things the rest of the world's technology stack cannot run without:
memory chips and battery cells. Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix supply a large share of
global DRAM and NAND; LG Energy Solution, Samsung SDI and SK On build the cells inside
EVs and grid storage across three continents. Around those giants sits a deep bench of
KOSPI and KOSDAQ equipment, materials and test companies that rarely gets covered in
English.

Chip & Cell covers exactly that — and nothing else.

## What you'll find here

**Data pages, refreshed daily.** Every one of our 27 covered stocks has a
[fixed page](/semiconductor/) showing price, moving averages, 52-week position,
returns and (when available) foreign and institutional flows. The pages update
automatically after each Seoul close. Same URL every day — bookmark them.

**Deep dives.** One name at a time: what moved, what the flows say, and where the stock
sits inside its sector. On days when a covered name moves sharply, it jumps the queue.

**Weekly reviews.** Every Friday we wrap up the week across both sectors: winners,
losers, and how the KOSPI and KOSDAQ backdrop shifted.

## How to read this site

Start with the [semiconductor](/semiconductor/) and [battery](/battery/) sector tables —
they rank all covered names by 20-day return. Colors follow the Korean convention
(red up, blue down); definitions live on the [methodology page](/methodology/).

Nothing here is investment advice. We publish data and analysis so you can do your own
work — always verify against primary sources before acting.
