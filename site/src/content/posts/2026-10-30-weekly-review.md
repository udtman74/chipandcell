## Semiconductors: Memory Mega-Caps Decouple from Domestic Equipment

The semiconductor complex exhibited a pronounced bifurcation this week between leading memory producers and domestic equipment and component vendors. Heavyweight SK Hynix outperformed with an +11.88% five-day gain, supported by resilient sentiment surrounding high-bandwidth memory (HBM) supply chains. Integrated device manufacturer Samsung Electronics advanced +4.68%, while test socket manufacturer ISC rose +5.02%, reflecting steady institutional demand for high-end packaging and testing infrastructure.

Conversely, domestic equipment and proprietary component suppliers faced heavy selling pressure. High-pressure annealing specialist HPSP was the week's primary laggard, tumbling -10.75%. Precision test pin manufacturer Leeno Industrial shed -8.65%, and silicon carbide (SiC) ring producer TCK fell -8.46%. The stark divergence between the frontline memory manufacturers and their downstream supply chain underscores that broad-based capital expenditure confidence remains muted. With the median 20-day return across Korean semiconductor equities standing at -28.20%, this week’s memory rebound operated primarily as a narrow, large-cap stabilization rather than an industry-wide recovery.

## Rechargeable Batteries: Cell Manufacturers Diverge from Foil and Electrolytes

Korea's battery supply chain displayed similar polarization over the five-day period, against a backdrop of a -10.49% median 20-day return. Carbon nanotube (CNT) conductive additive supplier Nano Materials led all covered names with a +12.98% return, while cell manufacturer Samsung SDI rallied +11.66%, benefiting from a short-term rebound after extended sector-wide valuation compression. Further up the value chain, cathode producer POSCO Future M recorded a modest gain of +1.19%.

In sharp contrast, manufacturers tied to battery foils and specialty electrolytes bore the brunt of ongoing market de-risking. Lotte Energy Materials dropped -16.94% over the week, making it the sector's weakest performer, while copper foil peer SKC retreated -13.15%. Electrolyte salt and additive producer Chunbo also slid -6.81%. The steep underperformance in foil and chemical producers reflects persistent market concerns over sluggish European electric vehicle demand, protracted utilization rate adjustments, and inventory overhangs that continue to weigh more heavily on intermediate materials than on integrated cell makers.
