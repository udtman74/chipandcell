## Fundamental Context: CVD-SiC Consumables and Etch Demand

TCK is the primary pioneer of solid chemical vapor deposition silicon carbide (CVD-SiC) focus rings, holding an entrenched position as a core consumable supplier to tier-1 dry etch original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) such as Lam Research and Applied Materials, as well as end-memory manufacturers Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix. 

The primary company-specific catalyst behind TCK's recent relative strength centers on the operating inflection in memory fabrication:

1. **NAND Layer Scaling and HARC Etching Intensity:** CVD-SiC rings position directly around the silicon wafer during plasma etching to maintain plasma uniformity. As leading-edge 3D NAND transitions deeper into advanced multi-stack vertical architectures (200-to-300+ layers), High Aspect Ratio Contact (HARC) etching requires higher RF plasma power and longer etch step durations. Because higher power accelerates wear on consumables, unit consumption of high-density CVD-SiC rings increases per processed wafer relative to conventional silicon rings.
2. **Fab Utilization Recovery:** The +18.56% 20-day expansion tracks broader domestic semi strength (+16.32% peer median), consistent with rising wafer starts across Korean fab lines. Given that focus rings are replacement consumables tied to fab run rates rather than purely new greenfield capacity additions, TCK’s top-line leverage responds rapidly to rising fab utilization and maintenance spending.

The divergence in institutional buying (+178,201 shares) reflects confidence in consumable replacement volumes into late 2026, contrasting with earlier market concerns surrounding inventory digestion in the memory supply chain.
