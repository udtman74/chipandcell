2. **Upstream Synergies via Cosmo Chemical:** Investors are paying close attention to intra-group vertical integration. Cosmo AM&T sources raw materials and precursor integration through its affiliate, Cosmo Chemical, which operates domestic cobalt refining and battery recycling facilities. In an environment shaped by US Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) compliance and regional supply chain mandates, this local closed-loop linkage provides the company with a structural cost and regulatory edge over non-integrated mid-tier competitors.
3. **MLCC Release Film Buffer:** Unlike pure-play cathode producers exposed entirely to EV cell cycle swings, Cosmo AM&T maintains a high-margin functional film division. The company supplies specialized release films for multi-layer ceramic capacitors (MLCCs)—predominantly to key domestic accounts like Samsung Electro-Mechanics. An uptick in automotive electronics content and high-end IT component utilization provides cash-flow ballast that complements its cathode growth profile.
