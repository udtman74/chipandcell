## Company-Specific Drivers

SK Innovation’s sustained divergence from the broader Korean battery peer group points to fundamental developments unique to its corporate structure:

1. **Balance Sheet Stabilization via the SK E&S Merger:** Unlike pure-play battery competitors that remain exposed to external capital calls and heavy borrowing costs amid the EV demand plateau, SK Innovation fundamentally reshuffled its risk profile. Following the merger with highly profitable LNG and city gas affiliate SK E&S, finalized in late 2024, SK Innovation internalized an annual EBITDA run rate of over ₩1 trillion. This stable energy cash flow has progressively relieved market anxiety surrounding capital expenditure funding for subsidiary SK On, reducing the need for dilutive equity issuance or high-cost debt.

2. **SK On Operational Inflection and Capacity Ramps:** Market sentiment surrounding the battery division is shifting from cash-burn anxiety to capacity execution. With commercial operations scaling at joint-venture facilities—notably BlueOval SK sites with Ford and the dedicated JV plant with Hyundai Motor Group in the United States—investors are pricing in higher recognition of Advanced Manufacturing Production Credits (AMPC) under the U.S. Inflation Reduction Act (IRA). The sharp 60-day gain (+51.12%) indicates that the market is beginning to model volume-driven margin expansion for SK On entering late 2026.

3. **Resilience in Conventional Energy:** In contrast to pure-play battery cell manufacturers, SK Innovation's blended refining and petrochemicals exposure (via SK Energy and SK Geo Centric) acts as a baseline earnings buffer. Stable middle-distillate margins have prevented deep operating losses during battery demand troughs, providing valuation support that battery pure-plays did not enjoy over the past 20 sessions.
