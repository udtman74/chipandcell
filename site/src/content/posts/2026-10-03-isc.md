The company is the global market-share leader in silicone rubber test sockets, a segment that has gained structural share over traditional metallic pogo-pin sockets in high-speed, fine-pitch applications. 

The primary operational drivers behind ISC’s valuation profile include:

1. **Exposure to High-Performance Computing (HPC) and AI Silicon:** Silicone rubber sockets offer shorter electrical path lengths and lower contact resistance, making them technically necessary for testing high-bandwidth logic chips, including GPUs, server CPUs, and AI accelerators. As major fabless designers and foundries scale advanced packaging architectures, demand for ISC's flagship elastomer solutions has expanded beyond traditional mobile application processors.
2. **Post-Acquisition Integration with SK Group:** Following its acquisition by SKC in late 2023, ISC has functioned as a core semiconductor materials platform within SK Group. This relationship has accelerated joint commercialization efforts and cross-selling opportunities across domestic memory supply chains, complementing ISC's diversified customer roster across North American fabless majors.
3. **Consumable Replacement Velocity:** Test sockets are consumable components tied to both R&D tape-outs and high-volume production testing. Rapid architectural iterations in high-density computing sustain baseline order rates regardless of broader consumer hardware cycles.
