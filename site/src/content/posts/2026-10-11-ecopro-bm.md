## Fundamental Context and Company Drivers

EcoPro BM operates as South Korea’s largest pure-play cathode active material (CAM) manufacturer, with production focused heavily on high-nickel chemistries (predominantly NCA and NCM). Its commercial fortunes are structurally tied to two core captive relationships: Samsung SDI—serviced both directly and via their EcoPro EM manufacturing joint venture—and SK On.

The recent 20-day surge (+18.56%) points to two identifiable operational factors:

1. **Easing of Destocking Pressure and Inventory Losses**: The prolonged compression in cathode makers' margins was primarily driven by lag effects between falling lithium/nickel input prices and fixed-formula selling prices, which triggered repeated inventory valuation write-downs. With underlying metal benchmarks stabilizing, the market is pricing in an inflection in gross margins toward normalized conversion margins, particularly on high-nickel CAM shipments bound for North American EV platforms.
2. **High Operating Leverage on Utilization**: Because EcoPro BM expanded fixed capacity aggressively at its Pohang manufacturing hubs, small improvements in order releases from Samsung SDI and SK On produce disproportionate operating margin recovery. As downstream cell makers align their North American joint-venture ramps (such as StarPlus Energy with Stellantis) with OEM production timelines, order visibility has begun to stabilize relative to the troughs seen when the stock touched its 52-week low of ₩91,142.
