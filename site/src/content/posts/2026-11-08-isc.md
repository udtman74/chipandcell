## Fundamental and Structural Drivers

ISC’s price dynamics reflect its role as the global market leader in silicone rubber test sockets—a consumable hardware component critical for final package testing of high-performance computing (HPC) processors, AI accelerators, and mobile application processors (APs). Following its acquisition by SKC in late 2023, ISC positioned itself aggressively toward high-margin system semiconductor customers, shifting mix away from legacy memory sockets toward advanced packaging test solutions for tier-1 North American fabless and foundry clients.

Two main fundamental mechanisms help explain the magnitude of the recent move:

1. **High Operating Leverage to Advanced Packaging Cycles:** Silicone rubber test sockets command higher gross margins than conventional pogo-pin sockets, particularly in large-die AI accelerators where socket wear is elevated. When end-market chip packaging volumes face supply chain reassessments or customer inventory adjustments, consumable suppliers like ISC experience rapid order pushouts. Having traded up toward ₩271,000 on expectations of persistent AI server socket demand, the stock has experienced significant valuation de-rating as broader semiconductor hardware multiples contract.
2. **Sub-Sector Beta and Benchmark De-Risking:** With the KOSPI down 6.02% and the peer group median falling 41.24% over 20 days, systemic de-grossing by institutional portfolios has disproportionately impacted higher-beta hardware names. While ISC's 60-day return (-3.59%) demonstrates that the stock held up relatively well through late Q3, the rapid reversal over the past 20 sessions indicates that institutional selling accelerated once key technical supports—such as the MA60 at ₩173,758—were breached.
