2. **Specialty Analog and Compound Semi Tailwinds:** DB HiTek's strategic transition toward high-voltage PMICs and preliminary capacity allocation for compound semiconductors (silicon carbide/SiC and gallium nitride/GaN) provides insulation against commoditized silicon pricing.
