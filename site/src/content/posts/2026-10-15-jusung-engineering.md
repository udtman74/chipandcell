## Fundamental Context and Company Drivers

Jusung’s positioning and heightened volatility reflect its core exposure to leading-edge memory capital expenditure and recent corporate restructuring initiatives:

1. **Atomic Layer Deposition (ALD) Memory Exposure:** Jusung is a critical domestic supplier of high-k dielectric ALD equipment used primarily in advanced DRAM capacitor fabrication. Demand for its High-k ALD systems is tightly coupled with tech transitions at key domestic customers—predominantly SK Hynix—as memory makers advance 1b-nm and 1c-nm DRAM architectures to support High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) production. The run from the 52-week low of ₩26,450 toward the ₩283,000 peak was heavily underpinned by market expectations of expanding ALD tooling intensity per wafer in these sub-10nm class nodes.

2. **Corporate Restructuring and Valuation Repositioning:** Jusung's multi-fold re-rating over the past year has also been catalyzed by corporate actions aimed at spinning off its non-semiconductor business units (display and solar). The goal of this structural split has been to eliminate the conglomerate discount and position Jusung’s core equipment unit as a pure-play semiconductor ALD specialist. However, when macro sentiment deteriorates, stocks that have re-rated aggressively on structural narratives frequently face sharper institutional profit-taking, as evidenced by today's institutional outflow.

3. **Memory Capex Sensitivity:** Equipment suppliers sit at the volatile end of the semiconductor value chain. Today’s underperformance relative to broader market indices reflects broader investor anxiety over the cadence of 2027 memory wafer fab equipment (WFE) commitments, particularly outside dedicated HBM packaging lines where standard PC and mobile DRAM demand recovery remains uneven.
