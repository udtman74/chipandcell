## Fundamental and Operational Drivers

Lotte Energy Materials—rebranded from Iljin Materials following Lotte Chemical’s ₩2.7 trillion acquisition in early 2023—produces electrodeposited copper foil (elecfoil) for lithium-ion batteries. The sustained 60-day advance (+93.31%) and today’s breakout reflect several tangible operational factors:

1. **Malaysian Cost-Curve Advantage:** The market appears to be pricing in margin expansion derived from the company’s manufacturing base in Kuching, Sarawak. Electricity accounts for roughly 30–40% of copper foil manufacturing cash costs. The Malaysian facility utilizes low-cost, continuous hydroelectric power, giving Lotte Energy Materials a structurally lower cost floor than domestic Korean peers and European market entrants.
2. **Product Mix Shift toward High-End Foil:** Premium tier-1 cell manufacturers (including primary customer Samsung SDI) are shifting production toward high-nickel chemistries and large cylindrical formats (e.g., 46-series cells). These formats require ultra-thin, high-elongation, and high-tensile-strength foils to prevent tearing and swelling. Lotte’s high-end "I2B" foil products command higher processing premiums, insulating realization prices from base-metal fluctuations.
3. **Capacity Utilization Recovery:** After significant inventory destocking throughout the mid-stream EV battery chain, factory run-rates have stabilized. The 44.50% 20-day move reflects institutional expectations of sequential volume recovery in trailing utilization metrics compared to the lower baseline when the stock touched its 52-week low of ₩22,350.
