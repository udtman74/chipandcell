## Company-Specific Fundamentals and Sector Drivers

Jusung’s sharp drawdown reflects its high operational beta to global memory capital expenditure cycles. The company is a specialized vendor of Atomic Layer Deposition (ALD) tools, primarily deployed for high-aspect-ratio capacitor dielectric deposition in advanced DRAM nodes (such as 1b and 1c nanometer processes). 

Several fundamental factors explain the acute pressure on the stock:

1. **Memory Capex Sensitivity:** Equipment makers bear the brunt of any moderation in wafer fab equipment (WFE) commitments. With memory makers closely managing advanced DRAM and high-bandwidth memory (HBM) packaging budgets, order visibility for front-end ALD tools can deteriorate rapidly if node transitions or greenfield facility buildouts face scheduling adjustments.
2. **Customer Concentration and Domestic Exposure:** Jusung relies heavily on key domestic accounts—most notably SK Hynix—alongside secondary international business. When broad domestic equities experience severe liquidations (evidenced by the KOSPI’s 6.16% single-day plunge), high-beta suppliers to domestic memory giants experience outsized flow-driven selling.
3. **China Export and Demand Dynamics:** Beyond its domestic base, Jusung has historically maintained meaningful revenue exposure to Chinese semiconductor and display manufacturers. Ongoing export control uncertainties and lumpiness in Chinese trailing-edge memory investments introduce heightened volatility to Jusung's non-captive backlog.
4. **Corporate Restructuring Sentiment:** The company previously pursued corporate split initiatives to separate its core semiconductor equipment business from its display and solar divisions to unlock pure-play semiconductor multiples. In risk-off environments, conglomerate discounts or restructuring-related uncertainty often prompt institutional investors to trim exposure in favor of larger-cap, liquid alternatives.
