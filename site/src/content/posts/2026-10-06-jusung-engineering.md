## Drivers: Advanced ALD Adoption and Structural Re-Rating

The primary catalyst supporting Jusung’s outperformance is the structural demand for its Atomic Layer Deposition (ALD) technology, which remains central to cutting-edge memory manufacturing:

1. **High-Aspect-Ratio Capacitor Deposition in Advanced DRAM:** As domestic memory makers transition deeper into sub-10nm DRAM nodes and expand High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) production architectures, capacitor aspect ratios have increased dramatically. Jusung’s proprietary High-k ALD and Plasma-Enhanced ALD (PEALD) tools provide the uniform thin-film step coverage and low-leakage dielectric properties required for these designs, insulating the company from legacy capacity pullbacks.
2. **Corporate Restructuring and Pure-Play Valuation:** Jusung’s push to separate its legacy display and solar equipment operations from its semiconductor division has clarified its operating profile. Historically valued at a discount to specialized equipment peers due to display-market volatility, the market is pricing Jusung closer to pure-play global ALD vendors.
3. **Client Diversification Efforts:** Outside its anchor relationship with domestic memory fabricators such as SK Hynix, market expectations have risen around Jusung expanding its customer base to global foundries and non-captive Asian memory producers seeking alternatives to traditional Western toolmakers.
